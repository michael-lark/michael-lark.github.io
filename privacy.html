Privacy policy

assistant is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Drive files (read-only) and Gmail messages, to make a backup copy.
  • Where data goes: only to a server the owner controls at home. Nothing is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Other users: none. The tool is not offered to anyone else and collects no data about anyone else.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the local copy deleted by the owner.
